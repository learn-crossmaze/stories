How Stories works

A simple subscription: hold a few books at a time, swap them whenever you like, and never worry about a due date.

  1. 1. Join

    Sign up on this website, or at any branch. Choose your branch and a plan, then pay online or at the counter. The plan fee and the refundable deposit are shown separately before you pay.

  2. 2. Borrow

    Pick books from the shelves at your branch, or browse the catalogue in your online account and reserve a title. When a copy is ready, we let you know and hold it for you for a limited time (usually 48 hours).

  3. 3. Exchange as often as you like

    Return books and take new ones in the same visit, any number of times while your plan is active. Your plan sets how many books you hold at once, not how many you read.

  4. 4. At home, if you prefer

    With a plan that includes home delivery, book a visit from your online account: we bring your reserved books and collect the ones you've finished, in one trip. Each visit has a small fee, set by the branch.

Questions

Is there a due date or a late fine?

No. Keep a book as long as you're reading it. There are no fines for keeping books longer.

What happens when my plan ends?

You can still return books at any time. To borrow or reserve again, renew: a renewal made before your plan ends starts on the day it ends, so you don't lose any days.

Can children have a membership?

Yes. Everyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian. Children under 13 don't have a login of their own: the guardian's account manages their membership, books and reservations.

Can I change my plan?

You can upgrade at any time. The unused part of your current plan is credited towards the new one, which starts right away.

What is the deposit for?

Every member pays a refundable security deposit, kept separately from the plan fee. It's returned when you close your membership, less the cost of any book that's lost.

How do I hear about my books and plan?

Your branch can send WhatsApp updates: when books are issued or a reservation is ready, and before your plan ends. You can reply STOP to switch them off.
