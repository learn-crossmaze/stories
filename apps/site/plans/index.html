Good to know

  • Books at a time counts the books you have out, plus any copy we're holding for you after a reservation.
  • Pay for longer, pay less: quarterly, half-yearly and yearly options cost less per month where a plan offers them.
  • The deposit is refundable. It's kept separately and returned when you close your membership, less the cost of any book that's lost.
  • Renew early: a renewal starts the day your current plan ends, so you never lose a day.
  • Upgrade anytime: the unused part of your current plan counts towards the new one.
  • Home delivery is available with plans that include it, at branches that deliver; each visit has a small fee shown on the branch.

Prices shown are today's. Your branch confirms the plan when you join, online or at the counter.
